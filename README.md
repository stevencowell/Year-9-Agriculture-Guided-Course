# Year 9 Agriculture guided course

A complete, static student-learning course for Wagga Wagga High School's 2026 Year 9 Agriculture programme.

## Student destinations

- 19 two-week modules covering Introduction to Agriculture, Vegetable Production, Beef Production and Poultry Production
- 57 source-grounded named theory sections
- exactly 10 multiple-choice learning checks per named section (570 total)
- autosaving written evidence and Print / Save PDF
- 12-card folio with device-local backup/restore and optional photo records
- 21 varied Busy Work activities covering every named module topic
- 11 validated project-specific YouTube clips with privacy-enhanced playback and fallbacks
- confirmed 2026 assessment pathway and authoritative task links
- plan-reading theory plus the authorised cattle-handling diagrams with Open larger

## Source boundary

The authorised 2026 Google Drive folder is the primary local authority. The current NSW Agricultural Technology 7–10 Syllabus (2019) controls 2026 outcome alignment. Missing local plans, exact dates, Task 2 conflicts, Classroom codes and practical details remain **Teacher to confirm**.

Browser autosave is device-local evidence preparation, not cloud submission. Teacher-observed practical competence cannot be replaced by website entries, photos or student self-report.
