Course home

Browser autosave is device-local preparation, not cloud storage or submission. Keep original evidence files and follow current school privacy and submission directions.

Farm WHS and safe animal movement

Record one teacher-approved farm observation. Separate the observed condition, possible harm, general control principle and action referred to the teacher.

Evidence checklist
  • Observed condition, not an invented incident
  • Hazard and harm clearly connected
  • No unauthorised animal handling or local procedure claimed

Agriculture systems and enterprises

Model one agricultural enterprise using inputs, processes, outputs and connections to processing, transport or markets.

Evidence checklist
  • At least three connected system elements
  • One supported market or supply-chain connection
  • Current figures include a dated source or are omitted

Topic Test preparation

Use your completed modules and teacher-issued task document to identify three secure ideas, two ideas to revisit and one revision action.

Formal boundary: the Topic Test is teacher-issued. This folio card supports revision; it is not the test paper or an answer key.

Vegetable design and plant profile

Prepare a source-backed plant profile and explain how the harvested plant part, growth requirements and intended product influence production decisions.

Evidence checklist
  • Accurate plant structure and function
  • Source title and access date
  • No unsupported crop specification

Production diary and safety

Record a teacher-approved vegetable activity with date, conditions, observations, work completed, safety controls and the next monitoring step.

Evidence boundary
  • Record only work actually completed
  • Teacher directions control tools, products and rates
  • A photo does not replace required written evidence

Climate, harvest and financial evidence

Use dated evidence to explain one climate influence, one production or harvest result and one financial implication for the vegetable enterprise.

Evidence checklist
  • Observation separated from inference
  • Units and source recorded
  • No invented yield, cost or revenue

Packaging, market and Task 2 boundary

Explain how packaging or marketing could suit the intended product and customer. Use the teacher-issued task documents to organise confirmed report sections.

Teacher to confirm: the task and rubric define seven assessable sections, while other wording says all eight sections. The assessed outcome set also conflicts across the schedule, task and notification.

Beef feedlot research

Build a referenced explanation of a beef feedlot system, including purpose, inputs, management, welfare considerations and market connections.

Evidence checklist
  • Current sources named and dated
  • Fact, interpretation and judgement separated
  • No local school-enterprise details assumed

Beef practical preparation

Prepare for teacher-supervised cattle work by identifying purpose, general hazards, communication and evidence you can record without claiming competence.

Assessment boundary: formal practical competence is directly observed by the teacher. Self-report, photos and this page do not replace that judgement.

Poultry anatomy and development

Use an accurate labelled diagram or dated observation to connect poultry structure to function and explain one stage of embryonic development.

Evidence boundary
  • Observation is not diagnosis
  • Incubator settings remain teacher-controlled
  • Labels must match the authorised source

Poultry systems and enterprise evaluation

Compare broiler and layer enterprises using common criteria, then make a cautious judgement that considers welfare, biosecurity, markets, costs and sustainability.

Evidence checklist
  • Common comparison criteria
  • Gross margin not confused with whole-business profit
  • Current market claims are dated and sourced

Whole-course evaluation and source register

Select your strongest evidence, explain what it demonstrates, identify a limitation and improvement, and record the sources that support your final judgement.

Teacher to confirm: final submission route, required file format, photo/privacy rules and whether any current farm-site or construction plan must be included.
