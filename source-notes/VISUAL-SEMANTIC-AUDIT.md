# Visual semantic audit

## Scope and provenance

- Seventeen standalone SVG assets were authored one at a time in Steve's signed-in ChatGPT session: one landing hero, four term learning maps and twelve folio evidence cards.
- Every SVG includes a meaningful `<title>` and `<desc>`, uses a fixed `viewBox`, and has no external fonts, scripts, raster images or web dependencies.
- The visual system uses the shared eucalyptus, leaf, ochre, soil, cream, sky and charcoal palette recorded in `VISUAL-MANIFEST.json`.

## Source-boundary check

- Visuals teach concepts, observation and evidence structures only. They do not claim a local farm layout, specific breed, dimensions, capacities, dates, marks, assessment rules or experimental results.
- Local practical activities, procedures, materials and missing assessment details remain visibly labelled `Teacher to confirm` where represented.
- Diagrams are learning maps and folio prompts, not authoritative construction plans or pseudo-data charts.

## Render audit

- All seventeen SVGs were parsed and rendered at their native aspect ratio, then reviewed as a complete contact sheet and at larger size.
- The landing hero was revised so the course title, topic chips and safety line occupy separate rows without collisions.
- Folio card 05 was revised so safety-record rules do not touch labels and its image/caption panel has clear insets.
- The wider pass corrected smaller edge, clipping and label-spacing issues in the remaining term maps and folio cards.
- Final result: no visible clipping, label collision, unreadable overprint or unsupported visual claim remained in the audited set.

## Release decision

The visual package is suitable for integration. The asset-by-asset title, purpose, alt text, provenance and source boundary are recorded in `VISUAL-MANIFEST.json`.
